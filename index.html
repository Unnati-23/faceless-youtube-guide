The setup

Pick your niche

Start with one. You can add more once you have a process that works.

Educational explainers

History, science, mysteries, “why does this happen”, random topics explained. Calm, curious narration that keeps people watching.

Motivational videos

Comebacks, discipline, short speeches. The voice has to carry real emotion, from defeated to fired up.

Story channels (horror, true crime, “the house had been empty for years…”) work too. They need the most dramatic voice work of all.

02
The setup

Research with AI

Let AI gather the facts and angles, then check the key claims yourself before you script.

COPY-PASTE PROMPT / RESEARCH
Act as a research assistant for a faceless YouTube channel in the [NICHE] niche. My topic is: [TOPIC].
Give me:
1. Five surprising facts or angles most videos miss, each with a source I can check.
2. The one question viewers most want answered.
3. A simple story structure: hook, build-up, twist or payoff, ending.
Flag anything uncertain. Don't invent statistics, quotes or sources.
03
The setup

Write the script

Ask for a script with the voice direction built in, so it’s ready to paste straight into Drama 3.

COPY-PASTE PROMPT / SCRIPT
Using the research above, write a [60-second / 8-minute] faceless YouTube script in the [NICHE] niche.
- Open with a hook in the first 5 seconds.
- Short spoken sentences. No headings or stage notes outside brackets.
- Add voice direction for Fish Audio Drama 3 in [square brackets], written the way you'd direct an actor, e.g. [calm, quiet storyteller], [sharp breath], [whispering], [building, fired up].
- Change the direction wherever the mood changes, even mid-sentence.
- After the script, list one visual idea for each line.
04
The setup

Generate the visuals

Turn each visual idea from your script into an image or clip prompt. Keep one consistent style across the whole video.

COPY-PASTE PROMPT / VISUALS
Turn this list of visual ideas into image-generation prompts for a faceless YouTube video.
Style for every prompt: [e.g. cinematic, moody lighting, 16:9, no text, no real people's faces].
For each one, give: the scene, camera angle, lighting, and one detail that adds atmosphere.
Keep characters and colours consistent across all prompts.
Visual ideas: [PASTE LIST]

The most important step

Direct the voice with Drama 3

VOICE PROMPT / CALM → TERRIFIED
[calm, quiet storyteller] The house had been empty for years. [sharp breath, suddenly terrified, whispering] Until the lights turned on.
VOICE PROMPT / DEFEATED → FIRED UP
[defeated, tired, barely above a whisper] I failed again today. [deep breath, building with every word, fired up] But tomorrow, I'm coming back stronger.
VOICE PROMPT / CURIOUS EXPLAINER
[warm, curious, like telling a friend a secret] There's a reason you can't remember being a baby. [slower, lowering voice] And scientists only recently figured out why.

Tip: describe the feeling, not the sound. “[barely holding it together]” works better than “[sad voice]”.

The smart part

Sell it as a freelance service

Make a couple of sample videos in your niche, then pitch them to creators who already run faceless channels.

  • Build a sample reel: two or three short videos that show off the voice work.
  • Find creators: look for faceless channels that post often in your niche. They’re the ones who need help keeping up.
  • Offer clear packages: for example, script + voice, or a full video ready to upload.
COPY-PASTE PROMPT / PITCH
Write a short, friendly pitch message to the creator of a faceless YouTube channel called [CHANNEL] in the [NICHE] niche.
I make faceless videos as a freelancer: research, script, AI visuals and emotive AI voiceover.
- Mention one specific thing I like about their channel: [DETAIL].
- Offer to make one sample video on a topic that fits their channel.
- Under 90 words. No hype, no fake stats, end with a simple question.
